## ❓ Anticipated Questions & Answers

**Q: How is this different from existing POS systems?**
A: Traditional POS just records transactions. CartAI turns transactions into intelligence—recommending to customers, alerting on stock, forecasting demand, and answering business questions in plain English.

**Q: Will small shopkeepers actually use AI?**
A: They don't need to understand AI. They just ask questions like talking to a smart assistant: "What should I reorder?" No technical knowledge required.

**Q: What about the cost?**
A: < ₹5,000 for setup. Uses existing cameras and phones. No specialized hardware. Enterprise systems cost 10-100× more.

**Q: What if camera doesn't work?**
A: We have a "Quick Pick" fallback—select products manually from a list. Same experience afterward.

**Q: Is the AI reliable?**
A: Customer AI uses keyword-based responses (no API failures). Business AI can use real APIs or local models. Both are demo-ready and production-viable.

**Q: Why would they trust the recommendations?**
A: Because it shows the data: "87% of YOUR customers buy X with Y." It's their own store data, not a black box.
